## 1. Context & Objectives

Currently, posts across channels are overly trimmed down or lack the platform's native formatting strengths. 

**Goals:**
Calibrate `scripts/repurpose.py` to write copy that maximizes each platform's verified character limit and engagement mechanics:
- **X (Twitter Free Tier):** 240 – 275 characters (hard ceiling: 280). Punchy hook + 2 bullet points.
- **Bluesky:** 260 – 295 characters (hard ceiling: 300). Technical insight with byte-slice facets.
- **Meta Threads:** 350 – 480 characters (hard ceiling: 500). Conversational context + technical question for developers.
- **LinkedIn:** 1,200 – 2,500 characters (hard ceiling: 3,000). Deep engineering case study:
  - High-impact headline
  - The architectural bottleneck
  - Concrete solution & systems design
  - 3 actionable takeaways
  - Bottom line & verified attribution
